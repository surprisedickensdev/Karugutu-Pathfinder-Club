Core Pillars of Our Pathfinder Club

We foster well-rounded young individuals through dedicated spiritual, social, and physical mentorship.

Faith

Spiritual Growth

Deepening our relationship with Jesus Christ through daily personal prayer, Morning Watch devotionals, and scripture study for the Pathfinder Bible Experience (PBE).

Outreach

Community Service

Living our faith through direct Christian action. Our Pathfinders participate in church and town clean-up, visitation of the elderly, and helping families across Karugutu and Ntoroko.

Skills

Lifestyle Development

Fostering outdoor discipline and practical life skills: camping, knot tying, fire building, camporees, precision marching drills, and first aid honors.

Pathfinder Commitment

"By the Grace of God, I Will Be Pure, Kind, and True"

Learn the 8 Pathfinder Laws, explore our Aim and Motto, and sing along with the official Pathfinder Anthem on our About Us page.

Spiritual Growth in Pathfinders

Biblical Foundations

"Let no one despise your youth, but be an example to the believers in word, in conduct, in love, in spirit, in faith, in purity." — 1 Timothy 4:12

Key Activities in Karugutu Club:

  • Morning Watch: Daily personal prayer and devotional Bible reading.
  • Pathfinder Bible Experience (PBE): Deep, verse-by-verse scriptural mastery and regional competitions.
  • Evangelistic Efforts: Cadets preaching, singing, and sharing the Advent message in local communities.

Community Service & Outreach

Serving in Christ's Name

"For I was hungry and you gave Me food; I was thirsty and you gave Me drink; I was a stranger and you took Me in..." — Matthew 25:35

Our Outreach Programs in Karugutu:

  • Hospital & Home Visitation: Singing comforting hymns and praying for the sick.
  • Sanitation & Hygiene Drives: Cleaning town centers, water sources, and church grounds.
  • Food & Clothing Distribution: Helping widows, orphans, and displaced families in Ntoroko district.

Lifestyle & Skills Development

Mental & Physical Excellence

Pathfindering balances intellectual vigor, health habits, team discipline, and survival skills.

Key Practical Skills:

  • Drills & Marching: Precision marching, slow match, flag handling, and brass band cadences.
  • Camping & Knot Tying: Tents, shelters, knots, camp cooking, and outdoor survival.
  • Health & First Aid: CPR, wound dressing, temperance, and physical fitness.
